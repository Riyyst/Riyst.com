The Mission

Welcome to riyst.com — a growing hub where you can discover genuinely useful tools and resources for everyday life, productivity, and a little fun along the way.

I’m Riyst. I’m learning to code and I'm sharing that journey along the way by building tools I often need day‑to‑day. I also make a few fun 'tools' to keep things fresh. Alongside my own projects, I feature hand‑picked sites created by others that have a lot to offer.

What You Can Expect

  • Privacy‑first — I favour tools that respect your data and avoid invasive tracking. Everything is secure and private. No sign up required, and no tracking.
  • Reliable — Simple, dependable utilities that work when you need them.
  • Curated — A mix of my own builds and quality external tools for general users and developers.
  • Useful & fun — From productivity boosters to playful experiments. You're guarenteed to find something to play with!


The Roadmap

This site is always evolving. I plan to keep expanding with new utilities and, in time, add an Articles section to cover fresh digital and tech news across a range of topics and sub‑sections — helping you stay current without the noise.
